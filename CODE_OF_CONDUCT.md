# Code of conduct

Cascade welcomes students, educators, historians, accessibility reviewers, and software contributors. People deserve a respectful, useful experience regardless of their background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when discussing people, history, and code.
- Critique ideas and implementation choices without attacking the person who proposed them.
- Welcome good-faith corrections, questions, and requests for accessibility improvements.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and its educational purpose.
- Respect the privacy of students and other contributors. Do not post personal or classroom information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, including student work or identifying details, without consent.
- Deliberately disrupting project discussions or review.
- Using project spaces to promote hateful, violent, or otherwise abusive conduct.

## Moderation and reporting

The repository maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, and restrict participation when necessary to keep the project usable. To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub. If a private route is unavailable, open a brief issue asking for a private contact route and omit names, screenshots, and sensitive details.

Reports will be reviewed in context. This policy does not promise a response time, and it does not replace platform reporting tools for urgent threats or abuse.
